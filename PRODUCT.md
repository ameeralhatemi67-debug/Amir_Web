## Product Purpose

## Stack

Astro with fully static output. Vanilla JavaScript and CSS for the desktop (`src/os`), drawn at build time and taken over in the browser. English and Arabic routes, native RTL, dusk/morning/system themes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Every project, Work (filterable), Films, Lab, About, Contact, Privacy and a 404 have their own address in both languages. The desktop also has Settings, Search, a Terminal, keyboard shortcuts and window management. No database, environment secrets, analytics or contact form. Keep source projects read-only. Push and deploy only when the owner asks. No invented biographical facts, clients, metrics or release status.
