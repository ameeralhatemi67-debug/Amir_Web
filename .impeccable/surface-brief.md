## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is the evidence. A concise personal introduction leads immediately into large screenshots and an uneven gallery. Reject the oversized two-line name, homepage placeholder portrait block, fake device renders, and repeated text rows. The separate About page retains the small, labeled photo placeholder expressly requested in the original brief.

OWN-WORLD: A crisp mineral-white canvas and deep green ink, warm and cool accents reserved for interaction. Real application colors belong inside the images. A typographic name replaces the invented monogram. Variable Manrope remains, with a tighter, restrained scale and readable Arabic pairing.

STORY: Recognize Amir and his focus, see real work, open an honest case study, and reach a professional profile. Distinguish a public deployment capture from a source-project design-review capture.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead and short introduction. The featured application's real public lecture-archive screenshot begins in the first viewport, with a compact caption and case-study link. On phones the title and action sit above a full-width image; the name never consumes a screen.

FORM: A work-first gallery explicitly selected by the user. Grounded candidate 3, a software project contact sheet, is the assigned form, seed 9331ca19. Other considered forms were a product catalog, project log, research poster, reading index, studio shelf, and developer notebook. The catalog challengers were declined because the owner pinned real screenshots and a gallery; keep their commitment to one focal composition, not their visual motifs.

INTERACTION: A restrained image focus on hover and keyboard focus, 180ms ease-out, no layout movement. A single short initial introduction reveal from an already-visible state, 350ms ease-out. All motion disabled under reduced motion. No scroll-hiding content or parallax.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
